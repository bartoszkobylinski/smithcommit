Let CommitSmith Work Its Magic!

Oh, weary coder, you've come to the right place! The legendary CommitSmith is here to transform your diff message into a beautiful commit message that will make your fellow developers smile.

Just follow these simple steps, and let the magic happen:

  1. First, summon the diff from your Git repository by casting the powerful incantation git diff > your_file.txt in your command-line terminal. This spell will conjure the diff and store it in a text file.
  2. Open the enchanted text file you've just created and behold the diff message within.
  3. Cast your eyes upon the mystical text input field below, where your diff message shall be entered.
  4. Type or paste your diff message into the enchanted box, and watch as it eagerly awaits CommitSmith's touch.
  5. When you're ready to witness the magic, press the "Give Me My Beautiful Commit" button, and let CommitSmith weave its spell.
  6. Behold the result! A commit message crafted with love, humor, and a sprinkle of CommitSmith's secret ingredient.
  7. Copy your beautiful commit message, and use it in your repository. Share the joy and wonder of CommitSmith with your fellow coders!

So, are you ready to embark on this magical journey? The CommitSmith is eager to assist you!
